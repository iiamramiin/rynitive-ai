Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to my data?

Your CSV is processed entirely in memory, analyzed for PII patterns, and immediately deleted. We don't store, log, or share your data. The PDF report is emailed to you and deleted after 24 hours.

Can I use this for HIPAA or clinical data?

No. This tool is for de-identified datasets or non-PHI data only. It provides automated indicators, not HIPAA compliance certification. For regulated clinical data, consult your compliance team.

How accurate is the PII detection?

We use Microsoft Presidio with ~85-95% accuracy on common PII types. However, no automated tool is perfect. The report flags uncertainty and recommends manual review for critical use cases.

What file formats do you support?

Currently CSV only, up to 20MB. Excel (.xlsx) and JSON support coming soon based on demand.

What if I'm not satisfied?

100% money-back guarantee. If the report doesn't help you make better decisions, reply to the delivery email within 24 hours for an immediate refund, no questions asked.

Do you offer bulk pricing?

Yes! For 10+ datasets per month, contact us for volume discounts (up to 50% off) or unlimited monthly plans starting at $99/month.

Can this replace legal review?

Absolutely not. This tool provides automated scanning only, not legal advice or compliance certification. Final data sharing decisions remain your responsibility.

How long does analysis take?

Most CSV files are analyzed in 10-30 seconds. You'll receive your PDF report via email within 60 seconds of payment.
